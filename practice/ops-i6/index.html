B  Short Answer

Outline, Explain

Answers could include:

  • Australian Consumer Law: product safety, acceptable quality, guarantees
  • Environmental protection laws: emissions, waste
  • Brief effect of each on operations

C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

Smart Toys Co designs children's toys in Melbourne and has them made overseas. It had to recall a toy because small parts were a choking hazard. It has no product testing process, and its overseas factory has never been inspected.

Task: Recommend operations strategies to make sure Smart Toys Co meets its legal obligations.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Identifies Australian Consumer Law and product safety standards
  • Recommends testing before launch (quality assurance) and supplier inspections or audits
  • Recommends design changes that meet safety standards
  • Explains the cost of compliance vs the cost of recalls and reputation damage

D  Case Study Collector

Describe  |  Outcome H7

Business: A product recall on productsafety.gov.au

Research and add to your Operations Case Study Bank:

  • The product and the business
  • What the safety problem was, and the date
  • Which legal requirement applied
  • How the business changed its operations

Use a reliable source from the last 3 years where you can. Record the source name and date.

Check your entry: what to look for
  • A real, dated recall from the official site
  • Correct link to the Australian Consumer Law or a safety standard
  • Clear operations response (redesign, supplier change, testing)
