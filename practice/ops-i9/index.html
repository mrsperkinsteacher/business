Answers could include:

  • Lower risk of fines, boycotts and future regulation

C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

Fresh Farm Eggs supplies cage eggs to cafés across the Hunter region. Cafés are switching to free-range suppliers, and local residents complain about the smell and truck noise from its farm.

Task: Recommend how Fresh Farm Eggs can improve its environmental and social responsibility.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Identifies changing community and customer expectations
  • Recommends moving towards free-range or higher welfare production, possibly in stages
  • Recommends community actions (odour management, truck timing, consultation)
  • Weighs costs against keeping customers and the licence to operate

D  Case Study Collector

Analyse  |  Outcome H7

Business: Kathmandu

Research and add to your Operations Case Study Bank:

  • Two environmental or social practices in Kathmandu's operations
  • One fact (e.g. B Corp score), with source and date
  • How these practices affect its operations
  • Whether they give it an advantage

Use a reliable source from the last 3 years where you can. Record the source name and date.

Check your entry: what to look for
  • B Corp certification, recycled materials, supplier code of conduct
  • Dated evidence from a reliable source
  • A judgement linked to competitive advantage
