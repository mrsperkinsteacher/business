C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

Wild Coast Surfboards sells boards online across Australia. Deliveries take 10 days, two key suppliers are in China, and stock is tracked on a spreadsheet that is often wrong.

Task: Recommend supply chain strategies for Wild Coast Surfboards.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Identifies slow logistics, supplier risk and poor stock information
  • Recommends an inventory management system linked to the online store
  • Recommends a logistics partner or faster delivery options
  • Recommends a back-up supplier and weighs local vs global sourcing

D  Case Study Collector

Explain  |  Outcome H7

Business: Amazon

Research and add to your Operations Case Study Bank:

  • How Amazon manages logistics
  • One fact about its Australian operations, with source and date
  • How e-commerce drives its supply chain
  • One criticism or risk

Use a reliable source from the last 3 years where you can. Record the source name and date.
