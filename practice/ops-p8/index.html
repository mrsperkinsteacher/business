C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

PowerPro makes cordless drills. Its one-year warranty is shorter than competitors' three years, and 8% of drills are returned with battery faults. Sales have dropped this year.

Task: Recommend how PowerPro should use warranties and quality management to improve its operations.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Identifies the battery fault and short warranty as problems
  • Recommends finding the cause (supplier, design) and fixing quality first
  • Recommends extending the warranty once quality improves, to signal confidence
  • Notes consumer guarantees under the Australian Consumer Law still apply
