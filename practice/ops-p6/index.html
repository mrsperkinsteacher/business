B  Short Answer

Outline, Explain

1. Outline the link between monitoring, control and improvement. (3 marks)

Write your answer in your book first. Then open the marking guide and mark it yourself.

Show the marking guide
MarksWhat it looks like
3Clear, accurate answer using an example or correct terms

Answers could include:

  • Monitoring measures actual performance against KPIs
  • Control compares results with targets and corrects problems
  • Improvement uses what was learned to make processes better over time

2. Explain how KPIs help a business improve its operations. (4 marks)

Write your answer in your book first. Then open the marking guide and mark it yourself.

Show the marking guide
MarksWhat it looks like
4Clear cause and effect, with a relevant example
3Sound answer, less detail or no example

Answers could include:

  • Give clear, measurable targets
  • Show where performance falls short
  • Allow corrective action and track improvement over time
  • Example KPIs (defect rate, on-time delivery, customer satisfaction)

C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

QuickFix Phone Repairs has five stores and promises same-day repairs, but 30% of repairs are late. Nobody records how long each repair takes or why some phones come back with the same fault.

Task: Recommend how QuickFix can monitor, control and improve its operations.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Identifies the lack of monitoring and data
  • Recommends KPIs (repair time, on-time %, return rate) and a recording system
  • Recommends control actions when targets are missed (staffing, parts stock)
  • Recommends continuous improvement (staff suggestions, regular review)
