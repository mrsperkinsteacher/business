C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

Corner Kitchen is a busy restaurant. Cooks walk long distances between the fridge, stove and serving area. Every cook does every task, orders are written on paper, and meals take 35 minutes to reach the table.

Task: Recommend changes to technology, task design and layout to speed up service at Corner Kitchen.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Identifies poor layout, no specialisation and slow order technology
  • Recommends redesigning the kitchen layout so stations follow the cooking steps
  • Recommends specialised stations (grill, cold, plating)
  • Recommends a digital ordering system and setting a target time

D  Case Study Collector
