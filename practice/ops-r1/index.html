B  Short Answer

Outline, Explain

1. Outline the difference between cost leadership and good/service differentiation. (3 marks)

Write your answer in your book first. Then open the marking guide and mark it yourself.

Show the marking guide
MarksWhat it looks like
3Clear, accurate answer using an example or correct terms

Answers could include:

  • Cost leadership: being the lowest-cost producer in the industry so the business can charge lower prices or earn higher margins
  • Differentiation: making the good or service different or better (features, design, quality, service) so customers will pay a premium
  • Clear contrast made, ideally with an example of each (e.g. ALDI vs a premium brand)

2. Explain how an operations strategy can help a business sustain its competitive advantage. (4 marks)

Write your answer in your book first. Then open the marking guide and mark it yourself.

Show the marking guide
MarksWhat it looks like

3Sound answer, less detail or no example
2Describes with little explanation
1Some relevant information

Answers could include:

  • Names a specific operations strategy (e.g. JIT, quality management, technology, supply chain)
  • Shows cause and effect: the strategy lowers costs or improves quality/speed/flexibility
  • Links this to cost leadership or differentiation
  • Explains why the advantage lasts (hard to copy, continuous improvement, ongoing investment)

C  Mini Business Report

Recommend, Justify  |  Outcomes H8, H9

Harbour Bikes is a Sydney bicycle manufacturer with 40 staff. Imported bikes are selling for half its price. Its bikes are well made but look much like the imports, and it has no clear selling point. Profits have fallen for two years.

Task: Recommend whether Harbour Bikes should pursue cost leadership or good differentiation. Justify your recommendation with at least two operations strategies.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Recognises it cannot easily win on cost against cheaper imports (scale, labour costs)
  • Recommends differentiation (e.g. custom builds, premium materials, local repairs and service, e-bikes) with clear reasons
  • Supports it with operations strategies: quality management, new product design, customisation, customer service
  • Notes the risk or cost (e.g. higher costs, R&D spending) and how to manage it

Business: ALDI Australia

Research and add to your Operations Case Study Bank:

  • What ALDI does in its operations to keep costs low

Use a reliable source from the last 3 years where you can. Record the source name and date.

Check your entry: what to look for
