B  Short Answer

Answers could include:

  • Legal compliance: meeting the minimum standards set by law
  • Ethical responsibility: voluntarily doing what is right, beyond the law
  • Example showing the difference

  • Supply chain ethics (labour conditions, sourcing) sit within operations
  • Reputation and customer trust depend on how products are made
  • Investor and regulator pressure
  • Example of a business

C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

Urban Threads is an Australian fashion label. A news story claims a factory it uses overseas pays very low wages and has unsafe conditions. Urban Threads has broken no Australian law, but sales have dropped 15% since the story ran.

Task: Recommend how Urban Threads should respond, using the difference between legal compliance and ethical responsibility.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Explains that it is legally compliant but not meeting ethical expectations
  • Recommends supplier audits, a supplier code of conduct and transparency (publishing factory lists)
  • Recommends working with the factory to improve conditions, or changing suppliers
  • Weighs the extra cost against the reputation and sales benefit

D  Case Study Collector

Analyse  |  Outcome H7

Business: Nike

Research and add to your Operations Case Study Bank:

  • What labour problems Nike faced in its supply chain in the 1990s
  • One change Nike made, with source and date
  • Whether this was legal compliance or ethical responsibility
  • How the issue affected its operations

Use a reliable source from the last 3 years where you can. Record the source name and date.

Check your entry: what to look for
  • Sweatshop criticism of the 1990s
  • Responses such as factory lists, codes of conduct, audits
  • Correct classification as ethical responsibility (beyond the law)
