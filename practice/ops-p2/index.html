C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

Hilltop Bakehouse supplies bread to cafés. Its ovens are 20 years old and break down every week. Two experienced bakers have left, and the new staff have had no formal training.

Task: Recommend how Hilltop Bakehouse can improve its transforming resources.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Identifies both facilities (ovens) and human resources (skills) as problems
  • Recommends a maintenance plan and replacing or leasing ovens
  • Recommends structured training and ways to keep skilled staff
  • Explains the effect on output, quality and dependability

D  Case Study Collector

Describe  |  Outcome H7

Business: Royal Flying Doctor Service

Research and add to your Operations Case Study Bank:

  • Its human resources (types of staff)
  • Its facilities (aircraft, bases, equipment)
  • How these work together to deliver the service
  • One fact, with source and date

Use a reliable source from the last 3 years where you can. Record the source name and date.

Check your entry: what to look for
  • Correct identification of human resources and facilities
  • Links to the service delivered
  • Dated source
