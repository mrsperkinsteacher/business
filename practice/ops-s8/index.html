C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

Riverside Packaging plans to install robots on its packing line. Staff fear job losses, the union has raised concerns, and the robots will cost $1.5 million plus retraining.

Task: Recommend how Riverside Packaging can overcome resistance to this change.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Identifies sources of resistance: financial costs, retraining, redundancy fears, inertia
  • Recommends consultation with staff and the union, and clear communication
  • Recommends retraining and redeployment instead of redundancies where possible
  • Recommends a staged roll-out and managing the cost (leasing, finance)
