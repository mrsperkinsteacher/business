C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

Sunrise Internet has 5,000 customers. Phone calls wait 40 minutes, complaints have doubled this year, and customers are leaving for competitors. Staff have no written guide for common problems.

Task: Recommend operations strategies to improve customer service at Sunrise Internet.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Identifies long waits and inconsistent support as the problem
  • Recommends more staff at peak times, call-back systems and online help
  • Recommends training and written service standards
  • Recommends KPIs (wait time, complaints, satisfaction) to monitor results
