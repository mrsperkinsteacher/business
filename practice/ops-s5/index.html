B  Short Answer

Distinguish, Explain

1. Distinguish between leading-edge and established technology. (3 marks)

Write your answer in your book first. Then open the marking guide and mark it yourself.

Show the marking guide

Answers could include:

  • Leading edge: newest, most advanced, expensive, higher risk, possible first-mover advantage
  • Established: proven, reliable, cheaper, widely used
  • Example of each

2. Explain factors a business should consider when choosing between leading-edge and established technology. (4 marks)

Write your answer in your book first. Then open the marking guide and mark it yourself.

Show the marking guide

  • Cost and available finance
  • Strategy (cost leadership vs differentiation)
  • Risk and reliability
  • Staff skills and training needs

C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

MediLab runs pathology tests for local doctors. A new AI-based testing machine could halve test times, but it costs $2 million and has not yet been used in Australia. Its current machines are reliable but slow.

Task: Recommend whether MediLab should invest in leading-edge technology.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Identifies the trade-off: speed and advantage vs cost and risk
  • Makes a clear recommendation (e.g. trial or staged adoption)
  • Considers finance (leasing), staff training and approval
  • Justifies with likely effects on performance objectives

D  Case Study Collector

Analyse  |  Outcome H7

Business: Tesla

Research and add to your Operations Case Study Bank:

  • What leading-edge technology Tesla uses in its factories
  • What went wrong with automation in 2018, with source and date
  • How Tesla responded
  • What this shows about leading-edge technology

Use a reliable source from the last 3 years where you can. Record the source name and date.

Check your entry: what to look for
  • Model 3 production problems linked to too much automation
  • Elon Musk's admission that excessive automation was a mistake
  • Clear lesson about risk
