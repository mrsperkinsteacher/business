Influences - technology

Do A Quick Check first. Then choose one of B, C or D. Write your answers in your book or note guide.

2. Robotics on a production line most improves:

B  Short Answer

Outline, Explain

1. Outline how technology has influenced operations in ONE industry. (3 marks)

Write your answer in your book first. Then open the marking guide and mark it yourself.

Show the marking guide
MarksWhat it looks like
3Clear, accurate answer using an example or correct terms
2Mostly accurate, limited detail
1Some relevant information

Answers could include:

  • Names an industry (e.g. retail, manufacturing, banking)
  • Gives a specific technology (robotics, AI, online ordering, automation)
  • States the effect on operations (speed, cost, quality, staffing)

2. Explain ONE advantage and ONE disadvantage of investing in new technology for operations. (4 marks)

Write your answer in your book first. Then open the marking guide and mark it yourself.

Show the marking guide
MarksWhat it looks like
4Clear cause and effect, with a relevant example
3Sound answer, less detail or no example
2Describes with little explanation
1Some relevant information

Answers could include:

  • Advantage: faster, more consistent output, lower unit costs, better data
  • Shows how the advantage happens (cause and effect)
  • Disadvantage: high cost, retraining, redundancies, breakdowns or obsolescence
  • Shows the effect of the disadvantage on the business

C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

Greenvale Print is a family printing business using machines that are 15 years old. Jobs take three days, while online competitors deliver in 24 hours. The owners are worried about the cost of new machines and whether staff can learn new systems.

Task: Recommend how Greenvale Print should respond to changes in technology.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Identifies technology as the key influence and the gap with competitors
  • Recommends specific technology (digital printers, online ordering system)
  • Addresses cost (leasing, staged purchase) and staff (training)
  • Explains expected benefits for speed and competitiveness

D  Case Study Collector

Explain  |  Outcome H7

Business: Coles (automated distribution centres)

Research and add to your Operations Case Study Bank:

  • What technology Coles uses in its distribution centres
  • One fact or statistic, with source and date
  • How it affects speed, cost or staffing
  • One problem or risk with the technology

Use a reliable source from the last 3 years where you can. Record the source name and date.

Check your entry: what to look for
  • Automated warehouses and robotics in distribution centres
  • Dated evidence (opening dates, investment amounts)
  • Clear link between technology and operations performance
