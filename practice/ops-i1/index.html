C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

Southern Steelworks makes steel building frames in Newcastle. Imported frames from Asia are now 25% cheaper. Its customers are builders who want lower prices, but who also value reliable delivery and Australian standards.

Task: Recommend operations strategies Southern Steelworks could use to respond to global competition.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Identifies globalisation (cheaper imports) as the key influence
  • Recommends cost strategies: technology, automation, economies of scale, global sourcing of some inputs
  • Recommends differentiation: dependability, speed of local delivery, quality certification
  • Justifies with likely effects and notes a risk

D  Case Study Collector

Examine  |  Outcome H7

Business: Apple

Research and add to your Operations Case Study Bank:

  • Where Apple designs and where it assembles products
  • One recent change to its global supply chain, with source and date
  • One benefit and one risk of its global operations
  • How globalisation shapes its operations strategy

Use a reliable source from the last 3 years where you can. Record the source name and date.

Check your entry: what to look for
  • Design in the USA, assembly through contract manufacturers in Asia
  • A dated example of supply chain change (e.g. moving some assembly to India or Vietnam)
  • Benefit: low cost, scale. Risk: disruption, reliance on one region, labour standards
