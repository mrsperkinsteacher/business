Inventory management - advantages and disadvantages of holding stock, LIFO (last-in-first-out), FIFO (first-in-first-out), JIT (just-in-time)

Outcomes: H2, H5

Do A Quick Check first. Then choose one of B, C or D. Write your answers in your book or note guide.

A  Quick Check

Identify  |  tap an answer for instant feedback

1. Stock arrives only when it is needed for production. Which method is this?

2. A supermarket sells its oldest milk first. Which method is this?

3. A disadvantage of holding large amounts of stock is:

4. The biggest risk of JIT is:

B  Short Answer

Outline, Explain

1. Outline ONE advantage of holding stock. (2 marks)

Write your answer in your book first. Then open the marking guide and mark it yourself.

Answers could include:

  • Able to meet sudden rises in demand / avoid stock-outs
  • Bulk-buying discounts or protection against supplier delays

2. Explain how JIT could affect a business's costs and its dependability. (4 marks)

Write your answer in your book first. Then open the marking guide and mark it yourself.

Show the marking guide
MarksWhat it looks like

Answers could include:

  • Costs: lower storage, insurance and wastage, less cash tied up
  • Dependability risk: production stops if suppliers are late
  • Needs reliable suppliers and accurate forecasting
  • Example (e.g. Toyota)

C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

Crumb and Co is a 12-store bakery chain. It throws out about 20% of its bread each week. Its storeroom is full, yet some stores run out of flour.

Task: Write a short report to the operations manager recommending an inventory strategy.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Identifies both overstocking (waste) and stock-outs (poor control)
  • Recommends FIFO for perishable stock and better forecasting
  • Recommends JIT-style ordering for flour with reliable suppliers, or a small safety stock
  • Recommends an inventory system and KPIs (waste %, stock-outs)
