B  Short Answer

Write your answer in your book first. Then open the marking guide and mark it yourself.

Answers could include:

  • Goods are tangible, can be stored and are produced before they are sold
  • Services are intangible, cannot be stored, and are produced and consumed at the same time
  • Example of each, or a business that offers both

2. Describe the features of operations management for a business in a tertiary industry. (4 marks)

Write your answer in your book first. Then open the marking guide and mark it yourself.

Show the marking guide
MarksWhat it looks like
4Clear cause and effect, with a relevant example
3Sound answer, less detail or no example
2Describes with little explanation
1Some relevant information

Answers could include:

  • High customer contact (visibility): the customer is often a transformed resource
  • Output cannot be stored, so capacity must match demand (variation in demand)
  • Staff skills and customer service are central to quality
  • Technology used to speed service (online booking, self-service)

C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

Coastline Physio is a physiotherapy clinic with three sites on the Central Coast. Waiting times are long, patients say appointments feel rushed, and bookings are still made by phone only. Two physiotherapists have resigned this year.

Task: Write a short report to the practice owner recommending operations strategies to improve this service business.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Identifies service features: high visibility, cannot store output, staff are central
  • Recommends online booking and scheduling technology to cut waiting times
  • Recommends staffing strategies (recruitment, retaining staff) and customer service standards
  • Includes a way to monitor improvement (KPIs such as wait time, patient satisfaction)

D  Case Study Collector

Describe  |  Outcome H7

Business: Commonwealth Bank (or another tertiary business)

Research and add to your Operations Case Study Bank:

  • Main services it provides
  • How customers are involved in the service (visibility)
  • One technology it uses in operations, with source and date
  • How it manages customer service

Use a reliable source from the last 3 years where you can. Record the source name and date.

Check your entry: what to look for
  • Correctly identifies it as a service business in the tertiary sector
  • Describes inputs, transformation and outputs for a service
  • Uses a current, dated example (e.g. app features, branch changes)
