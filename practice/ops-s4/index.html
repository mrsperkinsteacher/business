C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

Clearwater Pools builds pools on the Central Coast. It does its own design, digging, tiling and accounts. The accounts are often wrong, and tiling is slow because its two tilers are overworked.

Task: Recommend which operations Clearwater Pools should outsource, and which it should keep in-house.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Identifies core (design, building) and non-core (accounts) activities
  • Recommends outsourcing accounts and possibly some tiling to contractors
  • Recommends keeping design in-house as a source of advantage
  • Notes risks (quality, control) and how to manage them (contracts, checks)
