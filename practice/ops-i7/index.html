B  Short Answer

Outline, Explain

1. Outline how environmental sustainability influences operations. (3 marks)

Write your answer in your book first. Then open the marking guide and mark it yourself.

Show the marking guide
MarksWhat it looks like
3Clear, accurate answer using an example or correct terms
2Mostly accurate, limited detail
1Some relevant information

Answers could include:

  • Customer, investor and government pressure to reduce environmental harm
  • Changes to inputs, energy, packaging and waste
  • Costs in the short term, savings and reputation benefits in the long term

2. Explain ONE advantage and ONE disadvantage for a business of making its operations more sustainable. (4 marks)

Write your answer in your book first. Then open the marking guide and mark it yourself.

Show the marking guide
MarksWhat it looks like
4Clear cause and effect, with a relevant example
3Sound answer, less detail or no example
2Describes with little explanation
1Some relevant information

Answers could include:

  • Advantage: reputation, customer loyalty, lower energy or waste costs, meets regulation
  • Cause and effect shown for the advantage
  • Disadvantage: high upfront cost, new processes, retraining
  • Cause and effect shown for the disadvantage

C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

Coastal Seafood Co processes fish on the NSW south coast. It uses large amounts of water and plastic packaging. Its main restaurant customers are now asking for greener options, and its local council is reviewing its waste-water licence.

Task: Recommend how Coastal Seafood Co can make its operations more sustainable.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Identifies environmental sustainability and regulation as influences
  • Recommends water recycling and waste reduction
  • Recommends switching to recyclable or compostable packaging
  • Weighs the costs against benefits (customers, licence, reputation)

D  Case Study Collector

Explain  |  Outcome H7

Business: IKEA

Research and add to your Operations Case Study Bank:

  • Two sustainability practices in IKEA's operations
  • One target or statistic, with source and date
  • How these practices affect costs or reputation
  • One criticism of IKEA's sustainability record

Use a reliable source from the last 3 years where you can. Record the source name and date.

Check your entry: what to look for
  • Renewable energy, sustainable timber sourcing, circular products (buy-back, repair)
  • Dated targets from IKEA reports or news
  • A balanced view including criticism
