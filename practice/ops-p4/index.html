C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

Rapid Renos completes kitchen renovations in Newcastle. Jobs often run two to three weeks late because tradespeople arrive in the wrong order and materials are not ready when needed. Customers are leaving bad reviews.

Task: Recommend how Rapid Renos can use sequencing and scheduling to complete jobs on time.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Identifies poor sequencing and scheduling as the cause
  • Recommends a Gantt chart for every job, shared with all trades
  • Recommends critical path analysis to identify tasks that cannot slip
  • Recommends ordering materials ahead and monitoring progress weekly

D  Case Study Collector

Analyse  |  Outcome H7

Business: Snowy 2.0 (Snowy Hydro)

Research and add to your Operations Case Study Bank:

  • What the project is
  • How far it has run over time and budget, with source and date
  • One cause of the delays
  • How better scheduling might have helped

Use a reliable source from the last 3 years where you can. Record the source name and date.

Check your entry: what to look for
  • Accurate, dated figures from reliable sources
  • Links delays to sequencing, scheduling or the critical path
  • A reasoned judgement
