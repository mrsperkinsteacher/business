C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

Koala Kitchenware sells only in Australia and buys its parts locally at high cost. It wants to expand into Asia, but its products are 40% more expensive than rivals.

Task: Recommend global operations strategies for Koala Kitchenware.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Identifies high costs and small scale as the problem
  • Recommends global sourcing of some parts
  • Explains how expansion could create economies of scale
  • Recommends scanning and learning (benchmarking Asian rivals) and some R&D to differentiate

D  Case Study Collector

Explain  |  Outcome H7

Business: Cochlear

Research and add to your Operations Case Study Bank:

  • How much Cochlear spends on R&D, with source and date
  • Where it manufactures and sells
  • How global factors help it compete
  • One risk of operating globally

Use a reliable source from the last 3 years where you can. Record the source name and date.

Check your entry: what to look for
  • R&D spending (roughly 12% of revenue)
  • Global markets and manufacturing sites
  • Link to scale, sourcing and R&D
