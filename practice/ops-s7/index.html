1. Distinguish between quality control and quality assurance. (3 marks)

Write your answer in your book first. Then open the marking guide and mark it yourself.

Show the marking guide

Answers could include:

  • Control: inspecting outputs against standards to detect defects
  • Assurance: systems and standards across the whole process to prevent defects (e.g. ISO 9001)
  • Example of each

C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

Summit Solar installs home solar panels. One in ten installations needs a return visit to fix faults. There are no written installation standards, and problems are only found when customers complain.

Task: Recommend a quality management approach for Summit Solar.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Identifies the lack of standards and reliance on complaints
  • Recommends written standards and checklists (quality assurance)
  • Recommends inspection after each job (quality control)
  • Recommends tracking faults and continuous improvement
