C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

Aussie Snack Co makes potato crisps. Sales are falling as customers choose healthier snacks. It has no research team and has not launched a new product in six years.

Task: Recommend how Aussie Snack Co should use new product design and development.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Identifies changing customer needs and no innovation as the problem
  • Recommends market research and a new product development process
  • Recommends prototypes and test marketing before full launch
  • Weighs the cost and risk against falling sales

D  Case Study Collector

Explain  |  Outcome H7

Business: Dyson

Research and add to your Operations Case Study Bank:

  • How Dyson develops new products
  • One fact (e.g. number of prototypes or R&D spend), with source and date
  • One successful and one unsuccessful product
  • How design gives Dyson a competitive advantage

Use a reliable source from the last 3 years where you can. Record the source name and date.

Check your entry: what to look for
  • Prototyping and R&D focus
  • A failure example (e.g. the cancelled electric car)
  • Link to differentiation
