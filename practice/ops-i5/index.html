C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

Kinetic Plastics makes food packaging in Western Sydney. New government targets require packaging to be recyclable, reusable or compostable within three years. None of its current products meet this, and its machines only work with one type of plastic.

Task: Recommend how Kinetic Plastics should adjust its operations to respond to this government policy.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Identifies government policy as the influence and the deadline as a risk
  • Recommends new product design using recyclable or compostable materials
  • Recommends new or upgraded equipment, possibly with grants or staged investment
  • Recommends staff training and a timeline with monitoring

D  Case Study Collector

Identify, Explain  |  Outcome H7

Business: An Australian business of your choice

Research and add to your Operations Case Study Bank:

  • ONE government policy that affected its operations in the last three years
  • Source and date
  • How the business changed its operations
  • Whether the change helped or hurt the business

Use a reliable source from the last 3 years where you can. Record the source name and date.

Check your entry: what to look for
  • A real policy, correctly named and dated
  • Clear link from the policy to an operations change
  • A judgement about the effect
