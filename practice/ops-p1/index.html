C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

Blue Gum Furniture makes timber dining tables in Gosford. Timber deliveries are often late and some boards arrive warped. This causes wasted time, wasted timber and customer returns.

Task: Recommend how Blue Gum Furniture can improve the management of its transformed resources.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Identifies materials (timber) as the problem input
  • Recommends supplier review or changing suppliers, with quality agreements
  • Recommends inspecting inputs on arrival (quality control)
  • Recommends better storage and scheduling of deliveries
