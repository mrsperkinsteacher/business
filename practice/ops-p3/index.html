OPS-P3  |  OPERATIONS PROCESSES

Transformation processes - the influence of volume, variety, variation in demand and visibility (customer contact)

Outcomes: H4, H5

Do A Quick Check first. Then choose one of B, C or D. Write your answers in your book or note guide.

A  Quick Check

Identify  |  tap an answer for instant feedback

1. A business producing a high volume of standard products usually has:

2. High variety in operations usually means:

3. Variation in demand refers to:

4. A restaurant where customers watch their food being cooked has:

B  Short Answer

Outline, Explain

1. Outline how volume and variety influence operations. (3 marks)

Write your answer in your book first. Then open the marking guide and mark it yourself.

Show the marking guide
MarksWhat it looks like
3Clear, accurate answer using an example or correct terms
2Mostly accurate, limited detail
1Some relevant information

Answers could include:

  • High volume: standardised, automated, low unit cost
  • High variety: flexible, skilled staff, higher unit cost
  • Usually an inverse relationship between the two

2. Explain how variation in demand and visibility affect operations in a service business. (4 marks)

Write your answer in your book first. Then open the marking guide and mark it yourself.

Show the marking guide
MarksWhat it looks like
4Clear cause and effect, with a relevant example
3Sound answer, less detail or no example
2Describes with little explanation
1Some relevant information

Answers could include:

  • Variation: capacity must change (casual staff, bookings, off-peak pricing)
  • Services cannot be stored, so peaks are hard to manage
  • High visibility: staff skills, presentation and customer service matter more
  • Example of a service business

C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

Snowline Ski Hire operates in the Snowy Mountains. Demand is huge in July but almost zero from November to May. Customers are fitted for boots in a small, crowded shop and often wait over an hour.

Task: Recommend operations strategies to manage variation in demand and visibility at Snowline Ski Hire.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Identifies high variation in demand and high visibility
  • Recommends casual or seasonal staff and online pre-booking and fitting
  • Recommends using the off-season (e.g. mountain bike hire, maintenance, online sales)
  • Recommends improving the customer area and service standards

D  Case Study Collector

Analyse  |  Outcome H7

Business: Perisher ski resort

Research and add to your Operations Case Study Bank:

  • Rate Perisher on each of the 4 Vs (high or low)
  • One fact showing variation in demand, with source and date
  • How Perisher manages its peaks
  • What it does outside the snow season

Use a reliable source from the last 3 years where you can. Record the source name and date.

Check your entry: what to look for
  • All four Vs correctly rated with reasons
  • Dated evidence of seasonal demand
  • Strategies linked to the 4 Vs
