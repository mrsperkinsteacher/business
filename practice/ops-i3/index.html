C  Mini Business Report

Recommend  |  Outcomes H8, H9

Bright Bean is a chain of eight cafés. Online reviews say the coffee tastes different in each store and some orders are wrong. Each café trains its own staff in its own way, and there are no written standards.

Task: Recommend how Bright Bean can meet its customers' quality expectations.

About 250 words, written as a business report. Headings: Introduction / Current situation / Recommendations / Conclusion.

Check your report: a high-level response would include
  • Identifies inconsistent quality as the problem and quality expectations as the influence
  • Recommends written standards and consistent training across all stores (quality assurance)
  • Recommends monitoring (mystery shoppers, review tracking, KPIs)
  • Considers technology (order system) to reduce errors
