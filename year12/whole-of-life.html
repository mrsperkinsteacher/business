For the Whole of Life

Year 12 is one year. You are made for the whole of life. Some words to hold on to as you work.

And we know that in all things God works for the good of those who love him, who have been called according to his purpose.ROMANS 8:28 (NIV) - OUR VERSE FOR THE YEAR

Not every week of Year 12 will feel good. This verse is a promise that nothing is wasted, including the hard weeks.

Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord, not for human masters.COLOSSIANS 3:23 (NIV)

Your study is not just for a mark. Doing it well, and doing it honestly, is an act of worship.

I can do all this through him who gives me strength.PHILIPPIANS 4:13 (NIV)
Be strong and courageous. Do not be afraid; do not be discouraged, for the Lord your God will be with you wherever you go.JOSHUA 1:9 (NIV)

Including the exam room.

For I know the plans I have for you, declares the Lord, plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future.JEREMIAH 29:11 (NIV)

Your ATAR is part of your story, not the whole of it.

Trust in the Lord with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding; in all your ways submit to him, and he will make your paths straight.PROVERBS 3:5-6 (NIV)
For God has not given us a spirit of fear, but of power and of love and of a sound mind.2 TIMOTHY 1:7 (NKJV)

For the night before a task.

Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest.MATTHEW 11:28 (NIV)

Rest is not giving up. Jesus invites it.

Faith in Business Studies

StewardshipGenesis 2:15 - God put people in the garden "to work it and take care of it". Look for it in Operations: environmental sustainability and corporate social responsibility.

Honesty and fairnessProverbs 11:1 - "The Lord detests dishonest scales, but accurate weights find favour with him." Think about it when we look at ethics, legal compliance and how businesses treat customers.

People matter more than profitWatch for it in Human Resources: how a business treats its workers says a lot about what it values.

Struggling? You do not have to carry it alone. Talk to Mrs Perkins, your PC teacher, the school counsellor or chaplain. See Relax and Recharge for more support.

For the Whole of Life: Life to the full

"I have come that they may have life, and have it to the full." John 10:10 (NIV)

Jesus offers a full life, not just a good result. Your marks matter, and you matter more.
