LOOK AFTER YOURSELF

Relax and Recharge

Rest is part of the plan, not a reward for finishing. A tired brain learns and remembers less.

Sleep is part of study

Your brain stores what you learnt while you sleep. Teenagers need about 8 to 10 hours a night. Staying up late to cram usually costs you more than it gains.

Try: screens off 30 minutes before bed on school nights.

Move your body

A walk, a swim, a kick of the footy or a dance in your room. Even 10 minutes of movement lifts your mood and helps you focus when you sit back down.

Try: a walk between study blocks instead of scrolling.

Take real breaks

Scrolling is not a break for your brain. Get up, get outside, get a drink of water, talk to someone in your house.

Try: a 5 minute break after every 25 minutes of study.

Eat and drink well

Regular meals and plenty of water help you think clearly. Energy drinks and skipping meals make the crash worse.

Try: a water bottle on your desk every study session.

Keep doing what you love

Sport, music, youth group, a part-time job, time with friends. Year 12 is not the year to drop everything. These keep you balanced.

Try: put one thing you enjoy in your week before you plan your study.

Be still

Five quiet minutes can reset a busy head. Pray, read a psalm, breathe slowly, or just sit outside without your phone.

Try: Psalm 23 or Matthew 11:28 when it all feels like too much.

If it is more than stress

Feeling low, anxious or overwhelmed for more than a few days is worth talking about. You are not a burden.

  • At school: Mrs Perkins, your PC teacher, the school counsellor or the chaplain.
  • headspace - support for young people aged 12 to 25, online and in person.
  • Kids Helpline - 1800 55 1800, any time, for people aged 5 to 25.
  • ReachOut - articles and forums for young people.
  • In an emergency, call 000.

For the Whole of Life: Rest

"He makes me lie down in green pastures, he leads me beside quiet waters, he refreshes my soul." Psalm 23:2-3 (NIV)

God built rest into the rhythm of life. Stopping is not falling behind. It is trusting that you are held.
