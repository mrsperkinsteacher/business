HSC STUDY IDEAS

HSC Study Ideas for Business

General study tips are on How to Study. These are the ones that matter most for HSC Business Studies.

Know the shape of the exam

The HSC exam is 100 marks in 3 hours, plus 5 minutes reading time.

Section I 20 multiple choice (20 marks, about 35 minutes). Section II short answers (40 marks, about 1 hour 15). Section III a business report from stimulus (20 marks, about 35 minutes). Section IV an extended response using a case study you have studied (20 marks, about 35 minutes).

Try: practise each section on its own, with the timer set to its time.

Learn the syllabus dot points, not just the textbook

Every HSC question is built from a syllabus dot point. If you can explain every dot point in your own words, nothing in the exam is new.

Try: open the Dot Point Finder, cover the answer and explain each dot point out loud.

Build a case study bank

Have at least one real business for each topic, with dates, figures and what the business actually did. Sections III and IV reward specific, dated evidence, not general statements.

Try: one page per business. What it does, three facts with numbers, and which dot points it shows.

Answer the verb

Identify, explain, analyse and evaluate all want different answers. Evaluate means you make a judgement and back it up. Underline the verb before you write a word.

Try: take one question and answer it three ways, as identify, explain and evaluate.

Practise the business report

Section III must be written as a report, with headings, an introduction, a body and recommendations. Students lose easy marks by writing it as an essay.

Try: set out the report skeleton from memory in 2 minutes before every practice.

Link the four topics

Operations, Marketing, Finance and Human Resources all depend on each other. Strong answers show the link, for example how a new technology in operations changes staff training in human resources.

Try: for any strategy you learn, write one sentence on how it affects each of the other three topics.

Keep up with business news

Recent, real examples make an answer stand out. Five minutes of business news a few times a week gives you current examples to use.

Try: when you read a story, note which topic and dot point it fits.

Mark your own work

After a practice question, mark it against the marking guidelines. Find what the top band answer has that yours does not, then rewrite one paragraph.

Try: send Mrs Perkins a clear photo of your practice answer with the date and heading.
